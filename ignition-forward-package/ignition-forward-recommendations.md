# Ignition Forward: Strategic Recommendations

## 1. HOW WE HELP PAGE — DESIGN TIGHTENING

### Current Issues

| Problem | Impact | Root Cause |
|---------|--------|------------|
| **Redundant content** | Cognitive overload, dilutes message | Service cards appear twice (white cards + ladder visualization) |
| **Hero is abstract** | Doesn't immediately communicate value | "Meet you where you are" is poetic but vague |
| **Ladder section competes with cards** | User confusion about which to click | Two different visual treatments for the same 3 services |
| **Principles section feels disconnected** | Breaks momentum toward conversion | Generic icons, no proof attached |
| **CTA is weak** | Low urgency, no specificity | "Not sure which is right?" admits confusion |

### Recommended Fixes

**1. Kill the redundancy — ONE service presentation, not two**
- Remove the white service cards section entirely
- Keep only the ladder visualization (it's more compelling and shows progression)
- This cuts page length by ~30% and sharpens focus

**2. Rewrite the hero to be concrete**

| Current | Recommended |
|---------|-------------|
| "We meet you where you are. Take you where you need to go." | "Three ways to build AI capability. Pick your entry point." |
| "Every client enters at their own pace and climbs the ladder." | "Most clients start with Edge. Some skip straight to implementation. We'll help you choose." |

**3. Make principles prove themselves**
- Add a micro-proof to each principle:
  - "Build WITH You" → "That's why 100% of our clients can run their systems without us after 90 days."
  - "Transfer Knowledge" → "Every engagement includes documented playbooks and team training."
  - "Measure Outcomes" → "We track revenue impact, not activity metrics."
  - "Start Small, Scale Fast" → "Average time from first call to first workflow: 3 weeks."

**4. Strengthen the CTA**

| Current | Recommended |
|---------|-------------|
| "Not sure which is right for you?" | "Know where you want to start?" |
| "Let's talk. We'll help you figure out..." | "Book a 30-minute fit call. We'll recommend the right entry point." |
| "Move Forward" button | "Book Your Fit Call" button |

**5. Add a "Quick Selector" above the ladder**
Three clickable cards:
- "I want to learn AI myself" → Highlights Edge
- "I need help building something" → Highlights Forward Deployed  
- "I need ongoing AI leadership" → Highlights Fractional

### CTA Design Rules

1. **Service pages** → Use service-specific verbs ("Apply," "Scope," "Schedule")
2. **Segment pages** → Reference the segment in the CTA
3. **Proof pages** → Reference the proof ("Build Your Own...")
4. **Generic pages** → Use "Start the Conversation" (not "Move Forward")
5. **Final conversion** → "Move Forward" is fine (it's the brand phrase)

## 3. OBJECTION HANDLING FAQ — SEO/GEO OPTIMIZED

### Strategic Approach

The FAQ should target **high-intent search queries** that indicate someone is evaluating AI consulting options. These queries also perform well for **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** because LLMs pull from FAQ-structured content.

### Recommended FAQ Structure

**Target Keywords (SEO):**
- "AI consulting for professional services"
- "fractional AI officer cost"
- "AI implementation for law firms"
- "how to hire AI consultant"
- "AI training for executives"
- "build vs buy AI systems"

**GEO Optimization:**
- Each answer should be 2-3 sentences (LLM-friendly length)
- Include specific numbers (prices, timelines, percentages)
- Use "we" voice to establish authority
- End with a differentiator

### Recommended FAQ Content

#### Q1: How much does AI consulting cost?
**A:** Our engagements range from $7,500 (Edge executive training) to $150K+ (Forward Deployed implementation). Most clients start with Edge to build AI fluency before committing to larger projects. Unlike traditional consultants, we transfer ownership—you won't need us forever.

*SEO target: "AI consulting cost," "AI consultant pricing"*

#### Q2: What's the difference between a fractional AI officer and hiring full-time?
**A:** A full-time Chief AI Officer costs $400K+ annually with a 3-6 month ramp. Our Fractional AI Officer starts at $8K/month with immediate impact. You get cross-industry insights from someone who's built AI systems across dozens of companies, not just one.

*SEO target: "fractional AI officer," "fractional CAIO," "hire AI executive"*

#### Q3: Can AI really work for professional services firms?
**A:** Yes—we've helped law firms, accounting practices, and consultancies improve conversion rates from 55% to 85% using AI-powered client intelligence. The key is building systems that augment expert judgment, not replace it. Your relationships stay yours; AI just makes them more informed.

*SEO target: "AI for law firms," "AI for accounting firms," "AI for consultancies"*

#### Q4: How long does it take to see results from AI implementation?
**A:** Edge clients have working workflows within 3 weeks. Forward Deployed projects deliver production systems in 8-16 weeks. We measure success by business outcomes—revenue, time saved, conversion rates—not by hours billed or features shipped.

*SEO target: "AI implementation timeline," "how long to implement AI"*

#### Q5: What if we've tried AI tools before and they didn't work?
**A:** Most AI failures come from starting with technology instead of workflows. We audit your actual work patterns first, then build AI that fits how you already operate. Every system we deploy has been tested in our own business—we don't ship experiments.

*SEO target: "AI project failure," "why AI projects fail"*

#### Q6: Do we need technical staff to work with you?
**A:** No. Edge is designed for executives with zero technical background. For Forward Deployed, our team handles the technical work while training yours. By the end of any engagement, your team can run and modify the systems independently.

*SEO target: "AI consulting for non-technical," "AI training for executives"*

#### Q7: How is Ignition Forward different from McKinsey or Accenture AI practices?
**A:** We're operators, not advisors. Every system we offer runs our own business first. We don't produce strategy decks—we build working systems and transfer ownership. Our clients can fire us after 90 days because they own the capability.

*SEO target: "AI consulting vs McKinsey," "boutique AI consulting"*

#### Q8: What industries do you specialize in?
**A:** We focus on expert-led businesses where judgment and relationships are the product: professional services (law, accounting, consulting), founder-led companies, fund managers, and PE portfolio operations. If your competitive advantage is your expertise, we can help you scale it.

*SEO target: "AI consulting for [industry]," "AI for expert businesses"*

### Implementation Notes

- Add this FAQ to the **How We Help** page (below the ladder, above the CTA)
- Also add a condensed version to **each service page** with service-specific questions
- Use `FAQPage` schema markup for rich snippets
- Each question should link to the relevant service page

## 4. PROFESSIONAL SERVICES COPY — HIGH-TRUST ADVISORY POSITIONING

### Problem with Current Copy

The current copy says "Law firms, accounting practices, and consultancies" — this is accurate but **leads with legal**, which:
1. Narrows perceived fit (what about wealth managers? M&A advisors? Management consultants?)
2. Triggers "legal-first" associations (compliance, risk, slow)
3. Misses the unifying thread: **these are all high-trust advisory relationships**

### Recommended Rewrite

#### Navigation Dropdown
| Current | Recommended |
|---------|-------------|
| **Label:** Professional Services | **Label:** Advisory Firms |
| **Description:** Law firms, accounting practices, and consultancies. | **Description:** Where trust is the product and relationships are the moat. |

#### Homepage Card
| Current | Recommended |
|---------|-------------|
| **Title:** Professional Services | **Title:** Advisory Firms |
| **Description:** Law firms, accounting practices, and consultancies who see AI as leverage. | **Description:** Partners and principals at high-trust firms who want to scale expertise without diluting quality. |

#### Page Hero
| Current | Recommended |
|---------|-------------|
| **Label:** Who We Work With | **Label:** Who We Work With |
| **Headline:** AI for Professional Services | **Headline:** AI for Advisory Firms |
| **Subhead:** Partners and principals at law firms, accounting practices, and consultancies who see AI as leverage—not threat. | **Subhead:** You built your firm on trust, judgment, and relationships. AI should amplify those—not automate them away. |

#### Page SEO Title/Description
| Current | Recommended |
|---------|-------------|
| **Title:** AI for Professional Services | **Title:** AI for Advisory Firms — Law, Accounting, Consulting & Wealth |
| **Description:** AI enablement for law firms, accounting practices, and consultancies... | **Description:** AI systems for high-trust advisory firms. Scale your expertise without scaling your headcount. Partners at law firms, accounting practices, consultancies, and wealth managers trust us to build AI that protects what makes them valuable. |

### Why This Works

1. **"Advisory Firms"** is broader than "Professional Services" — includes wealth managers, family offices, M&A boutiques
2. **"High-trust"** is the unifying thread — these firms all sell judgment and relationships
3. **"Scale expertise without diluting quality"** addresses the real fear — that AI will commoditize their value
4. **"Protects what makes them valuable"** is defensive positioning — we're not here to disrupt you

### Supporting Copy Changes

**Pain Points Section Header:**
- Current: "What's Holding You Back"
- Recommended: "The Tension You Feel"

**First Pain Point:**
- Current: "Client Relationship Management — Critical relationships scattered across email, CRM, and memory."
- Recommended: "Relationship Intelligence — Your best clients get your best thinking. But that thinking is trapped in your head, not your systems."

**Outcomes Section:**
- Add qualifier: "Results from advisory firm clients" (not generic "our clients")
