# CRO Analysis - Key Website Improvements

## Material Changes to Implement

### 1. Edge Positioning
- **Current:** Positioned as consulting
- **Should be:** "Executive AI Training" - a training product, not consulting
- **Pricing:** $7,500 (Standard) / $12,500 (Andrew-Led Premium)
- **Format:** 3 sessions × 3 hours = 9 hours over 2-3 weeks

### 2. Forward Deployed - Two Tiers
- **Mini:** $25K-$50K, 4-8 weeks, 1-2 focused implementations
- **Core:** $75K-$150K, 8-16 weeks, full AI transformation

### 3. Fractional AI Officer - Three Levels
- **Advisory:** 8-12 hrs/month, $8K-$12K/month
- **Active:** 20-30 hrs/month, $15K-$25K/month  
- **Intensive:** 40+ hrs/month, $30K+/month

### 4. The Service Ladder Concept
Edge → FD Mini → FD Core → Fractional
"Every client enters at Edge and climbs the ladder"

### 5. 80/20 Model Emphasis
- 80% proven core + 20% custom = "Bespoke at scale"
- This is the margin protection strategy
