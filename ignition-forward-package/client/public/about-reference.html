The Problem We Saw

Expert services are trapped in a linear model.

More expertise requires more experts. Slow. Expensive. Unscalable. We watched AI transform industries while expert-led businesses—law firms, consultancies, investment teams, fund managers—struggled to adapt.

The problem wasn't the technology. It was the implementation. Generic AI tools that don't understand how professionals actually work. Consultants who advise but don't deploy. Technology that creates more problems than it solves.

We built Ignition Forward to bridge that gap. Not with slide decks. With systems that actually run. AI infrastructure that amplifies expertise instead of replacing it.

When the world was falling apart in 2008, I was in the room at a $10B+ hedge fund. That experience taught me what's at stake when systems fail—and what it takes to build ones that don't.
