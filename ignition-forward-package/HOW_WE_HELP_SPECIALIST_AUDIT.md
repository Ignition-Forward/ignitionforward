## EDGE PAGE AUDIT

### Current State Assessment
- Hero: Two-column layout with pricing card
- Sections: Who Is This For → Process → Comparison Table → CTA
- Pricing: $4,500 prominently displayed
- Process: 3-step timeline (Discovery, Build, Refine)

### CRO Specialist Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **No urgency or scarcity** - Add limited availability messaging
2. **Comparison table buries Edge** - Edge should stand out more, not be equal-sized
3. **CTA is weak** - "Start Your Edge" doesn't convey immediate value
4. **No social proof on page** - Missing testimonials specific to Edge

**Improvements:**
- Add "Only accepting 3 new Edge clients this month" urgency
- Include mini-testimonial from an Edge graduate
- Add "What happens after you book" clarity section
- Show ROI calculator: "If Edge saves you 2 hours/week = $X/year"

### Visual Design Director Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **Pricing card lacks premium feel** - Needs more visual weight
2. **Process timeline is generic** - Needs distinctive visual treatment
3. **Comparison table is boring** - Needs visual hierarchy, not just a table
4. **No hero imagery** - Text-heavy hero needs visual anchor

**Improvements:**
- Add animated "9 hours" countdown visualization in hero
- Create visual journey map instead of basic timeline
- Transform comparison into visual "path selector" with clear recommendation
- Add subtle particle/data flow animation behind pricing card
- Use glassmorphism effect on pricing card for depth

### UX Strategist Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **No clear next step after reading** - User might leave without action
2. **Information architecture is flat** - All sections feel equal weight
3. **Mobile experience needs work** - Pricing card may be too complex
4. **No FAQ section** - Common objections not addressed

**Improvements:**
- Add sticky CTA that appears after scrolling past hero
- Create progressive disclosure for process details
- Add FAQ accordion addressing: "What if I'm not technical?", "What tools do you use?", "Can my team join?"
- Add "Is Edge right for me?" quick assessment quiz

### Content Strategist Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **Headlines are functional, not emotional** - Need more impact
2. **Benefits list is generic** - Could apply to any AI training
3. **No story or transformation narrative** - Missing the "before/after"
4. **Taglines feel disconnected** - "Your Edge, Amplified" needs context

**Improvements:**
- Lead with transformation: "From AI-curious to AI-fluent in 9 hours"
- Add "Before Edge / After Edge" comparison for a real client
- Include specific workflow examples: "Turn 3-hour research into 20 minutes"
- Add founder quote about why Edge was created

### CEO Perspective

**What's missing for a decision-maker:**
1. **No clear ROI articulation** - What's the payback period?
2. **No team scalability info** - Can I send my whole team?
3. **No comparison to alternatives** - Why not just use ChatGPT tutorials?
4. **No credibility markers** - Who has done this before?

**Must-haves:**
- "Companies like yours who've completed Edge" logos/names
- Clear ROI statement: "Average Edge graduate saves 5+ hours/week"
- Team pricing option or mention
- Link to case study or detailed success story

## FRACTIONAL AI OFFICER PAGE AUDIT

### Current State Assessment
- Hero: Single-column with benefits list
- Sections: Comparison → Typical Month → Engagement Models → Governance → CTA
- Pricing: $15-35K/month range
- Unique: Animated comparison, accordion engagement models

### CRO Specialist Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **Price range too wide** - $15K-$35K creates uncertainty
2. **No qualification step** - Visitors don't know if they're ready
3. **Engagement models are confusing** - Too many options
4. **No urgency** - Feels like "whenever you're ready"

**Improvements:**
- Add "Is your company ready for a Fractional AI Officer?" assessment
- Recommend ONE engagement model clearly (Active Leadership as sweet spot)
- Add "Current availability: 2 slots remaining for Q1"
- Include "Schedule a fit call" as primary CTA, not generic contact

### Visual Design Director Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **Hero is text-heavy** - No visual anchor or imagery
2. **Comparison animation is good but small** - Needs more prominence
3. **Typical Month section is boring** - 4 identical cards
4. **No visual representation of the AI Officer role**

**Improvements:**
- Add hero image: professional headshot or abstract "leadership" visual
- Create animated "org chart" showing where Fractional AI Officer fits
- Transform Typical Month into interactive calendar/timeline
- Add "Meet your potential AI Officer" section with team photos
- Use gradient backgrounds to differentiate sections more

### UX Strategist Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **Too much information upfront** - Overwhelming for first visit
2. **Accordion hides important pricing** - Users might not click
3. **No clear path for different buyer stages** - Explorers vs. ready-to-buy
4. **Governance section feels disconnected** - Not clear why it matters

**Improvements:**
- Add "Quick Overview" vs "Deep Dive" toggle for content depth
- Show all engagement models expanded by default, or use tabs
- Add "I'm exploring" vs "I'm ready to talk" dual CTAs
- Move governance into engagement model benefits, not separate section
- Add "What our Fractional AI Officers have built" portfolio section

### Content Strategist Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **"AI Leadership" is vague** - What does that actually mean?
2. **Benefits list is generic** - Same as any consultant
3. **No personality or voice** - Feels corporate
4. **Missing the "why fractional" story**

**Improvements:**
- Lead with specific outcome: "Your AI strategy, executed—not just planned"
- Add "A week in the life of your Fractional AI Officer" narrative
- Include specific deliverables: "Month 1: AI Roadmap. Month 3: First system live."
- Add founder perspective on why fractional model works better
- Include "What we won't do" section for clarity

### CEO Perspective

**What's missing for a decision-maker:**
1. **No board-ready materials** - How do I justify this to my board?
2. **No comparison to hiring** - What's the true cost difference?
3. **No exit strategy** - What happens when we outgrow fractional?
4. **No industry specificity** - Does this work for my industry?

**Must-haves:**
- "Board presentation template included" as a benefit
- Total cost comparison: Fractional vs. Full-time hire (include recruiting, benefits, ramp time)
- Clear graduation path: "When you're ready for full-time, we help you hire"
- Industry-specific case studies or mentions

## FORWARD DEPLOYED PAGE AUDIT

### Current State Assessment
- Hero: Two-column with team animation
- Sections: Problem/Solution → Team Roles → Timeline → Day in Life → Build WITH → CTA
- Pricing: Custom (not displayed)
- Unique: Three-Layer Ecosystem diagram, team visualization

### CRO Specialist Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **No pricing guidance** - "Custom" creates friction
2. **Too many sections** - Page is very long
3. **Problem/Solution section is dense** - Moved from homepage, may not fit
4. **No clear differentiation from competitors**

**Improvements:**
- Add "Typical engagement: $X-$Y" range for transparency
- Consolidate sections: merge Team Roles into Timeline
- Add "Why Forward Deployed vs. traditional consultants" comparison
- Include "Projects we've shipped" portfolio with screenshots
- Add "Request a scoping call" with expected timeline

### Visual Design Director Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **Team animation is too abstract** - Doesn't convey value
2. **Three-Layer Ecosystem diagram is text-heavy** - Needs visual treatment
3. **Timeline looks like every other consulting page** - Not distinctive
4. **Day in Life section is boring** - Just a list

**Improvements:**
- Replace team animation with actual team photos or video
- Create animated Three-Layer diagram with hover states
- Transform timeline into visual "journey map" with milestones
- Make Day in Life into interactive timeline with expandable details
- Add project screenshots or demo videos
- Use more visual contrast between sections

### UX Strategist Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **Page is too long** - Users may not scroll to CTA
2. **No quick summary for scanners** - All content is equal weight
3. **Problem/Solution section feels out of place** - Different tone
4. **No way to estimate fit or scope**

**Improvements:**
- Add "TL;DR" summary card at top with key points
- Create "Is Forward Deployed right for you?" qualifier
- Add floating CTA that follows scroll
- Include project scope estimator: "Tell us about your project"
- Move Problem/Solution to be more integrated or remove
- Add "See our work" gallery section

### Content Strategist Recommendations

**Critical Issues:**
1. **"Build WITH You" is overused** - Needs fresh angle
2. **Too much process, not enough outcomes** - What do clients get?
3. **Three-Layer Ecosystem is jargon-heavy** - Needs simplification
4. **No emotional hook** - Very rational/logical

**Improvements:**
- Lead with transformation: "From idea to production in 12 weeks"
- Add specific project examples: "We built X for Y, resulting in Z"
- Simplify ecosystem to: "Your expertise + Our systems = Compounding value"
- Include client quote about the experience, not just results
- Add "The Forward Deployed difference" manifesto section

### CEO Perspective

**What's missing for a decision-maker:**
1. **No budget guidance** - Can't plan without numbers
2. **No risk mitigation** - What if it doesn't work?
3. **No IP clarity** - Who owns what we build?
4. **No reference customers** - Who else has done this?

**Must-haves:**
- Pricing range: "Typical engagements: $50K-$200K over 12-16 weeks"
- Guarantee or milestone-based payment option
- Clear IP ownership statement: "You own everything we build"
- 2-3 named reference customers with contact option
- "What's included" vs "What's not included" clarity
