# Removed Elements Checklist

This document lists elements from the original homepage that were removed or modified during the redesign based on the Homepage Design Brief. These elements can be repurposed for other pages on the site.

---

## 1. Hero Section Changes

### Removed/Modified:
- **Original Headline**: "AI Enablement for Expert-Led Businesses"
- **Original Subheadline**: "We partner with expert-led businesses to build AI systems that amplify expertise, not replace it. Our 80/20 model delivers bespoke results at scale."

### Replaced With:
- **New Headline**: "Accelerate What Matters."
- **New Subheadline**: "We build AI systems for expert-led businesses. Including our own. Use AI to amplify your expertise."

**Suggested Use**: The original headline could work well on the **About page** or **How We Help overview page** as a section header.

## 2. 80/20 Model Visualization Section

### Removed:
The original homepage had a dedicated **80/20 Model visualization section** with:
- Visual diagram showing "80% Proven Core" + "20% Custom to You"
- Detailed explanation of the model
- Comparison to "Custom AI" (too expensive) and "SaaS" (too rigid)

### Current State:
This content now appears in the **"What Makes Us Different"** section as a brief card ("80/20 Customization") rather than a full visual breakdown.

**Suggested Use**: 
- **Maguire page** - Expand the 80/20 visualization there
- **How We Help overview page** - Add as a dedicated section explaining the methodology
- **Services pages (Edge, Fractional AI, Forward Deployed)** - Reference in each service's approach
