# Customer Journey Audit & Page Improvement Plan

## Executive Summary

This document maps all potential customer journeys through the Ignition Forward website and identifies specific improvements needed for each page to match the quality and sophistication of the homepage and the new interactive About page design.

---

## Customer Journey Mapping

### Journey 1: The Curious Executive
**Path:** Homepage → About → Contact
**Persona:** CEO/Founder who heard about IF and wants to understand who they are
**Critical touchpoints:** Hero messaging, credibility signals, team information

### Journey 2: The Problem-Aware Leader
**Path:** Homepage → Segment Page (e.g., Professional Services) → Service Page (e.g., Edge) → Contact
**Persona:** Managing Partner who knows they need AI help but unsure which service
**Critical touchpoints:** Pain point validation, outcome proof, pricing clarity

### Journey 3: The Solution Seeker
**Path:** Homepage → How We Help → Specific Service → Maguire (proof) → Contact
**Persona:** VP/Director tasked with evaluating AI solutions
**Critical touchpoints:** Service differentiation, case study depth, ROI evidence

### Journey 4: The Fund Manager
**Path:** Homepage → Fund Managers → Maguire → Contact
**Persona:** GP or Fund Manager looking for operational AI
**Critical touchpoints:** LP reporting pain points, fund-specific proof, confidentiality assurance

### Journey 5: The PE Operator
**Path:** Homepage → PE Portfolio → Forward Deployed → Contact
**Persona:** Portfolio Operations lead at PE firm
**Critical touchpoints:** Multi-company deployment, value creation metrics, speed to impact

## Success Metrics

After implementation, each page should:
1. Load in under 3 seconds on 3G
2. Score 90+ on Lighthouse accessibility
3. Have at least 3 interactive elements
4. Include at least 1 social proof element
5. Have clear CTA visible without scrolling on mobile
6. Follow the navy/navy-light/off-white color rhythm
